Sri Gangamma Thalli
Sthala Puranam

The Mother Who Guards Kuppam

Gangamma is revered as the Grama Devatha — a fierce yet compassionate form of Shakti who protects the town from illness, drought and misfortune.

Kuppam lies where Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka and Tamil Nadu meet, and devotees from all three states come to Amma. Her annual Ganga Jatara, held every May for about nine days, is one of the oldest and most important festivals of the town, with a tradition said to go back more than three centuries.

Traditions

How Amma Is Worshipped

Chatimpu

The Jatara begins with the traditional public announcement through the streets of Kuppam, inviting everyone to Amma's festival.

Veshams

Devotees fulfil their vows by taking on veshams (sacred disguises) and moving through the town in devotion to Amma.

Pongal & Saare

Families cook pongal at the temple and offer sarees, kumkum, turmeric and bangles to Amma as thanksgiving.
