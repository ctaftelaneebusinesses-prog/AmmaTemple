May · Every year

Kuppam Ganga Jatara

Amma's grand festival of about nine days. It opens with chatimpu and continues with veshams, pongal offerings, processions and the final visarjana.

Ganga Jatara

Srivari Saare Offered to Amma

During the annual jatara, the traditional Srivari Saare from TTD was offered to Goddess Gangamma at Kuppam by Chief Minister N. Chandrababu Naidu.

Special pujas and alankaram of Amma every Friday evening. Register at the temple office.
