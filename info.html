Before You Enter

Visitor Guide

✓ Do's

  • Wash hands and feet before entering
  • Leave footwear at the counter outside
  • Keep silence and stand in the queue
  • Offer kumkum, turmeric, flowers & coconut
  • Receive prasadam with your right hand
  • Keep the temple clean

✕ Don'ts

  • No photography inside the sanctum
  • No alcohol, tobacco or non-veg food
  • Do not touch Amma's idol
  • No phones near the garbhagriha
  • No pushing or loud talk in queues
  • No plastic or littering

Dress Code

Traditional attire is requested for all devotees.

Women
Saree, half-saree or churidar with dupatta
Men
Dhoti / pancha with shirt, or kurta-pyjama

Shorts, sleeveless tops and torn jeans are not permitted.
