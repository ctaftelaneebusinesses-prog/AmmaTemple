Sri Gangamma Ammavari Devasthanam, Kuppam

Sri Gangamma is the Grama Devatha of Kuppam — the mother who protects her village from illness, drought and every misfortune.

For more than three centuries devotees from Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka and Tamil Nadu have come to seek her blessings. Every May the town celebrates her with the nine-day Ganga Jatara, one of the oldest festivals of the region.

Kuppam Ganga Jatara

Amma's grand festival of about nine days. It begins with the traditional chatimpu announcement and continues with veshams, pongal offerings and processions through the town, drawing devotees from three states.
